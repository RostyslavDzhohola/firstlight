## Portus town (lane/portus-village)

The castle town around the Portus spawn (D38). Built by `tools/blender/portus/build.sh` from two
Quaternius kits. The archives are **not** in git; the script checks the SHA-256 below, extracts only
their `glTF/` folders and licence texts (`extract.py`: data only, no traversal) into a fresh temporary
folder, and Blender 5.2.1 reads them headless with `--disable-autoexec --factory-startup` as glTF and
PNG data. No kit script or `.blend` file is opened. Build tools: Blender, `@gltf-transform/cli` 4.5.1
from the pnpm cache (the script never downloads it), Python 3 (`-I`).

What we used and changed:

- **Village kit:** plaster and uneven-brick wall modules (straight, door, window variants), corners,
  doors, windows and shutters, balcony, floor, round-tile and wooden roof modules, the tower roof,
  chimney, supports, vine patches, wooden fence, crate and wagon props, and the textures of the
  brick, plaster, rock-trim, round-tile, uneven-brick, wood-trim, metal-ornament and vine-leaf
  materials. The modules were assembled into our houses, cottage, market hall, Hall of Firsts,
  gatehouse room and keep top; inner faces hidden in the closed volumes were removed; window glass
  made opaque.
- **Nature kit:** CommonTree_3/5, Pine_4/5 and Bush_Common_Flowers, lightened (bark welded and
  decimated, a share of the leaf cards dropped), with the bark, leaf, pine-needle and flower textures.
- **Packing** (`pack.py`): every prefab's geometry is merged into one GLB node per prefab and LOD
  with two materials; LOD1 swaps kit tile roofs for their hull shells, drops vine patches, and turns
  trees into crossed cards with impostors rendered from the trees. Textures are box-filtered to
  512 px layers (colour in sRGB, normals renormalised, leaf cutouts premultiplied and dilated) and
  stacked into two WebP strips; roughness maps are replaced by per-material constants. The GLB is
  meshopt-compressed and quantised (16-bit positions).

Our own work in these files (CC BY 4.0, "FIRSTLIGHT contributors"): the town layout, the custom
fortification geometry (curtain wall, round towers, gatehouses, castle gate, keep body), stalls,
paving and the small props built in `build.py`, the generated cloth palette and glow material, the
LOD shells and impostor cards, and (round 2, below) the four market houses and the three portal
gates. The build scripts are MIT.

## Round 2 buildings (lane/buildings-2)

The owner's picks from the 2026-10-15 design round (D-b2-1..5): the Portus market house and the
portal gate A, Waystation Nine (by our Opus 5.5 lane), and the portal gate B and the Hollowmere
stilt shelter (by our GPT-6 Astra lane). All of it is our own work, made for this project in
Blender from primitives, with no third-party files: CC BY 4.0 ("FIRSTLIGHT contributors") for the
geometry and colours, MIT for the scripts.

- `tools/blender/round2/opus/`: the Opus design-round scripts (`flkit.py`, `house.py`, `gate.py`,
  `outpost.py`, `props.py`, `render_piece.py`). Changed for the game: the house is sized to each
  Portus house plot (`house.configure`), Waystation Nine's "9" is a modelled numeral instead of a
  font, the gate's world tints gain Portus, its obelisks stand off the dais.
- `tools/blender/round2/astra/`: Astra's two GLBs, read as glTF data only (SHA-256 checked by
  `pieces.py` before use): `portus-portal-gate.glb` (890,744 bytes,
  `0d75d162cd363615e73f2c72187ee893501c53d3a2b896fe3d523ee4e5b8c270`) and
  `hollowmere-arrival-outpost.glb` (901,744 bytes,
  `b0d5c0d13147d2f82e686f332bb492fd95404ec4c24050ef5ffa33c3263fdbc3`). They don't ship; the built
  meshes do, inside the Portus and outpost GLBs above.
- `tools/blender/round2/convert.py` / `pieces.py`: each piece becomes one mesh with per-corner
  colours (Astra's palette atlas sampled per corner), on five `OK_*` material slots (matte, gloss,
  metal, glass, glow) over the plain white layer, with ambient occlusion baked into the colours
  (Cycles, an isolated scene with a ground plane), a little height grime and value noise.

## Vegetation (lane/vegetation)

Source: Quaternius, [Stylized Nature MegaKit Standard](https://quaternius.itch.io/stylized-nature-megakit),
CC0 1.0 Universal. Download supplied on 2026-10-12; licence supplied in `License_Standard.txt`.
Archive SHA-256: `298f6732b872e4cf7b30e6e7abf9641c7f6dc6b326df37ac089533ed7e3d58c9`.
The archive is not in git. `tools/blender/vegetation/build.sh` verifies it and rebuilds offline from
`SRC/download.zip`, extracted into a fresh temporary directory for each build. Blender imports
only that verified extraction; the temporary files are removed on exit. The pinned gltf-transform 4.5.1 CLI must already be in the
pnpm cache, or be provided through `GLTF_CLI`; the script never downloads it.

Only glTF, referenced geometry buffers and PNG textures are imported, in headless Blender 5.2.1
with `--disable-autoexec --factory-startup`. URI validation rejects remote paths and traversal.
No pack scripts or Blender files are run. The author-supplied CC0 grant covers these derivative
meshes and atlases; the build and rendering code is MIT. Existing CC0 packs are credited in this
registry rather than in the client software's `THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.md`; this pack follows that pattern.

Used: CommonTree, Pine, TwistedTree and DeadTree variants 1–3; Bush_Common,
Bush_Common_Flowers, Fern_1, Plant_1, Plant_1_Big, Plant_7, Plant_7_Big,
Flower_3_Group, Flower_4_Group, Mushroom_Common, Mushroom_Laetiporus and Rock_Medium variants 1–3.
Grass, normal maps and the other pack models are not shipped.

Changes: roots centred at the origin, heights normalized in metres and exported +Y up; imported
seams welded before decimation; near trees capped at 900 triangles and near small props at 320;
small-prop far LODs decimated to at most 32 triangles. Trees' far LODs use four triangles in two
perpendicular cards, baked from the full source meshes at two orthographic bearings. Textures are
packed into a 2048² mesh atlas and a 1536×1024 tree-impostor atlas. Foliage paint is neutralized for
client tinting. A vertex role mask separates leaves, bark and stone; impostors infer leaves from
neutral paint per pixel, with dead trees using bark throughout. All materials use double-sided
alpha cutouts at 0.5, with no transparency sorting. Meshopt compression and quantization follow
the character pipeline. PNG is retained because the existing loader/pipeline already supports it.
