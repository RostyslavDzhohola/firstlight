## Blocker

**B1. A wrong egg-puzzle answer spawns uncapped, permanent hostile NPCs, and they farm XP with no decay.**
- Code:
  - `apps/server/src/world/quests/eggs.ts:81,131,134` raises `spawnRequest` for each wrong E2 press (3 Hopbits) or E5 rope (2 Bonewalkers).
  - `apps/server/src/world/quests/QuestSystem.ts:842-860` spawns them with `createNpc(..., {roam: 8})`. It has no per-player or per-area cap and no despawn timer.
  - In `apps/server/src/world/npc/NpcSystem.ts:233+` these NPCs have `point: null` and `removeAt: 0`. Only ambient groups despawn.
- Scenario:
  - A free guest stands at the Highscore Hill cabinet and sends wrong presses. The only throttle is `allowOp`, 5 ops/s, so that is about 15 Hopbits/s.
  - After 60 s that is roughly 900 Hopbits. They stay after the guest dies and keep running AI.
  - Every client within 80 m receives their state patches (my estimate is on the order of 1 MB/s).
  - The same attack works at the Drowned Chapel with Bonewalkers.
- XP farm: `apps/server/src/world/npc/rewards.ts:97` keys repeat-kill decay as `x${n.id}`, unique per NPC. The −10% decay (floor 30%) never applies to egg NPCs, so killing them is an unlimited XP and coin faucet.
- Fix:
  - Cap live egg-spawned NPCs (for example 6 per player and 12 per body).
  - Give them a TTL of about 90 s via `removeAt`.
  - Lock the puzzle for about 15 s after a wrong answer.
  - Key decay on the egg id (`e${egg}`).
  - Add a test for the cap.

**B2. The client has no inventory UI, so most of the loot and drop loop is unusable.**
- Code:
  - The server handles `drop` and `move` at `apps/server/src/world/BodyRoom.ts:198-205`, but the client never sends them.
  - There is no `KeyI` binding. The backpack is only listed in the shop's sell tab and the wardrobe (`apps/client/src/ui/panels.ts:174,328`).
  - `giveItem` fills the hotbar first (`BodyRoom.ts:583-605`), regardless of item type.
- Scenario:
  - After a death, pickup puts the first 3 pile items into hotbar slots 1-3. A Wool Tuft can take a weapon slot.
  - Everything else goes to the backpack. That includes a T3 weapon, tonics, and anything bought after the hotbar is full.
  - Backpack items can't be equipped or used, because `use` and `equip` only reach the hotbar.
  - Junk can't be dropped. Only Juno's sell tab removes it, so "trade by dropping" (DECISIONS) is impossible.
  - SPEC §4.4 lists `I` for inventory.
- Fix (client only, server handlers exist): add a 20-slot inventory panel on `I` with swap/move to hotbar via `move`, plus `drop` and `use`.

## Should-fix

1. **Prism Bubble is sold and dropped but does nothing.**
   - `apps/server/src/world/combat.ts:212` refuses `bubble` in `fire`. `BodyRoom.use` (`BodyRoom.ts:545-569`) handles only tonics and Patchkit.
   - The client sends `use` for it (`CombatClient.ts:270`), and the server ignores it.
   - It costs 120/400/1000 coins in the shop (`items.ts` `buildShop`), and `DROP_WEAPONS` (`npcs.ts:582`) makes it about 1 in 15 weapon drops.
   - Fix: implement the shell (150/200/260 HP per 100 owner max HP, radius, duration, cooldown), or remove it from the shop and drops.

2. **Two droppable ids are not in the item catalogue.**
   - `PLANET_COSMETIC.meridian.id = "hard_hat_oversight"` (`npcs.ts:594`, `614`) and the Receiver roll `"windup_key"` (`npcs.ts:622`, `690`).
   - The catalogue ids are `hard_hat` and `wind_up_key` (`items.ts:42`, `63`).
   - I confirmed these are the only two bad ids. Players can't wear them, `sell` rejects them as worthless, and the name shows as the raw id.
   - Fix: rename the ids. Add a test that every drop id, boss roll, chest cosmetic and planet cosmetic exists in the catalogue. The current check at `packages/shared/src/content/npcs.test.ts:94` only covers ids starting with `tonic`.

3. **Patchkit heals 1.5× its stated amount, can't heal allies, and has no channel.**
   - `BodyRoom.ts:566` sets `perSec = heal×maxHp/2` for `2000 + rate×1000` ms. T1 heals 45% instead of 30%, T3 70% instead of 50%.
   - The server ignores `targetId` (content.md says ally in crosshair, strangers included), and damage doesn't cancel it.
   - An earlier Sonnet review flagged the 1.5× and it is still open.
   - Fix: duration equals `overS`, add a channel, resolve the ally target server-side.

4. **Hollow King solo protection fails at levels 19-20.**
   - Royal Judgement is a flat 276 true damage (`npcs.ts:521`, resolved at `boss.ts:737-748`). A level 19 or 20 player has 280 or 290 HP and survives it solo.
   - With Large Tonics (120 HP per 8 s) and Patchkits, a solo kill looks feasible.
   - `npcs.test.ts` only checks the lethality claim at the boss's own level, 14.
   - Fix: compute 120% of the highest participant's max HP, split among those inside. Test it at levels 14 and 20.
   - The Receiver's 348 exceeds the level-20 max of 290, so it is fine.

5. **Egg items are a coin faucet.**
   - Each egg item sells for 500 (`items.ts:79,109`) and can be reclaimed every life (`QuestSystem.claimEgg`). E1 and E2 are near the hub and need no level.
   - About 1,000 coins within minutes of rebirth, against the content.md model of about 1,648 coins total by level 12.
   - Fix: make egg items unsellable or low value (they are trophies and cosmetics). If the 500 is intended, record it as a decision.

6. **Onboarding is not implemented.**
   - SPEC §6.12 and the M7 done-when require a ~60 s skippable tutorial and training dummy, which gives 50 XP.
   - There is no client code. `onboardingDone` (`events.ts:136`, `QuestSystem.ts:748`) is emitted only by `quests-content.test.ts:69`.
   - The Spark medal is unobtainable, and the test passes because it fakes the event.
   - STATUS.md doesn't list this gap. Implement it, or record a decision to defer and remove Spark.

7. **Every player's position, HP and userId is sent to every client.**
   - `packages/shared/src/net/schema.ts:113` leaves `players` unfiltered, and `BodyRoom.updateViews` (`BodyRoom.ts:947`) filters only NPCs and piles. SPEC §5.2 specifies 160/180 m player views.
   - A modified client has a radar over all 40 players on a 1,000 m planet. The earlier Sonnet review (item 15) flagged this, and no D-entry records the deviation.
   - Measured with a throwaway script: 5.2 KB for the full 40-player state, 880 B per tick, about 17 KB/s per client with everyone moving. That is about twice the STATUS figure of 8-12 KB/s.
   - Fix: keep a roster map (name, level, party) unfiltered and view-filter the position, HP and weapon fields. Otherwise write the decision down.

8. **IP: the Meridian "Associates" look like stormtroopers or clones.**
   - `apps/client/src/world/npcs/models.ts:261-285` makes identical ivory-and-chrome troopers with red slit visors. Unit 7 is also ivory (`#E9E4D8`).
   - content.md §3.4 and §8.2 specify slate-grey with amber visors and say to keep Unit 7 visibly different. `npcs.ts:9-10` documents the departure.
   - Combined with identical clones, "Not a Clone" and the Vat Room of trooper pods, it echoes the clone-trooper archetype.
   - Fix: slate-grey plus amber visors per the brief, and make the Vat Room pods less Kamino.

9. **Test quality: the medal and egg tests don't test the real paths.**
   - Medal and egg tests in `quests-content.test.ts` emit synthetic bus events: `onboardingDone`, `propBroken`, fake `shot` broadcasts, and a hand-set `lastAttackAt`. That is how the Spark and cosmetic-id bugs passed.
   - There are no behaviour tests for Patchkit, Bubble, tonics, block, friendly fire, `drop` or `move`. Only prices are tested.
   - `combat.test.ts:123-155` mutates `RULES.LOGOUT_MS` and `RULES.LINGER_MS` and restores them after the last assertion, not in `finally`. One failure poisons later tests.
   - `combat.test.ts:136-141` asserts "not logged out yet" after a fixed 100 ms against a 300 ms timer, so it can flake under load. Other fixed-sleep patterns: `combat.test.ts:70,80,93`, `npc.test.ts:481-523`, `destruction.test.ts:248-262`. STATUS already records one load-flaky voice test.
   - Fix: wait on conditions, restore constants in `afterEach`, and add one contract test that every medal and mission has a production emitter.

## Nit

- **Content mismatches**
  - Riverstaff knocks back on every hit (`combat.ts:437`), but content.md says every 3rd.
  - Emberglass burn applies only to players (`combat.ts:556`), so it never burns NPCs.
  - Grenade fuse is 1.6 s at every tier (`combat.ts:293`), but content.md says 1.5 s and 1.4 s for tiers 2 and 3.
  - Scatterpipe is 6×7 pellets (`weapons.ts`), content.md says 7×6. Total damage is equal.
  - Fuel drums (content §2.2) don't exist.
- **Protocol drift**
  - `NET.REWIND_MS` is 200 but `combat.ts:12` uses 150, and `FireMsg.t`, `melee.t`, `input.t`, `use.targetId` and `travel.viaPad` are ignored.
  - `ClientMessages` and `ServerMessages` omit `charge`, `scope`, `move`, `denied`, `correct`, `travelOk`, `reborn`, `loggedOut`, `swing`, `npcKill`, and the `kb` fields on `hit`.
  - A zod rejection in Colyseus kicks the client, so contract drift would disconnect players.
- **Message rate**
  - `consumeAttack` sets `youDirty` on every shot (`combat.ts:180-188`). That resends the full 20-slot inventory, medals and missions, about 1 KB per shot.
  - `travel`, `client:info`, `questSync` and `ping` sit outside the 5/s buckets. Only the 60/s Colyseus cap bounds them.
  - `pvpKills` (`BodyRoom.ts:91`) is never pruned.
- **Spoilers and puzzles**
  - E7's keypad has no attempt lockout, and the egg solutions are in the repo (`secrets.ts:49`). Once the repo is public, every first-finder plaque can be claimed from the source.
  - Pile eviction (cap 1,000 per body) can evict real death piles when junk piles pile up.
- **Spawning and versions**
  - New characters can `/api/play` any body, because the travel-ticket check at `apps/server/src/app.ts:131` runs only when a character row exists.
  - There is no client/server protocol-version gate, even though clients auto-rejoin after deploys with stale JS.
- **Repo hygiene**
  - `tools/blender/characters/__pycache__/*.pyc` is committed.
  - `docs/reviews/firstlook-1.png` shows your Chrome bookmarks bar and profile avatar.
  - The review docs embed local absolute (`/Users/...`) paths, and DECISIONS and SPEC say "OASIS-style". Scrub these before the repo goes public.
  - `apps/client/package.json` still lists react, react-dom and zustand, and `vite.config.ts` uses `@vitejs/plugin-react`, though D25 says plain DOM.
  - zod, esbuild and tsx use caret ranges against SPEC's exact pins.
  - `sourcemap: true` ships source maps in production.
- **Asset base**
  - `apps/client/src/audio/bossLines.ts:10` reads `VITE_ASSET_BASE` and then appends `/v/voice-1/`. SPEC §10.1 defines that variable as ending in `/v/<buildId>`, so the path would double up if it is set on Vercel. It also has a different default from `config.assetBase`.
