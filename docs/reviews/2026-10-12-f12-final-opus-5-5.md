## Blocker

**N-B1. Two-packet teleport through the planet's centre.** Confirmed by probe: the helper's `move/origin-server`, re-run by me, plus my `origin-rejoin`.
- **Where:** `apps/server/src/world/movement.ts:111-147` (`validateMove`).
- **Cause:**
  - At (0,0,0), `groundAt` returns NaN. Every NaN comparison is false, so both the ground check (`:134`) and the height check (`:138`) pass.
  - The step is purely radial, so the charged distance is 0 (`:145-146`). The step back out to any surface point is also free.
- **Scenario:** a non-admin client sends (0,0,0), then any surface point. My re-run moved 490.9 m in about 360 ms with 0 corrections. That allows:
  - instant portal or boss-arena access;
  - grabbing any pile on the body;
  - escaping a fight;
  - hiding at the centre, out of reach, while still sending inputs.
- **Knock-on (`origin-rejoin`):** the saved position is `[0,0,0]`, and a rejoin goes through `snapToGround` (`BodyRoom.ts:1228`) to a NaN position. The next move is refused, so the character is stuck at NaN. A whistle at the centre also places the horse at NaN (`HorseSystem` `besideOf`).
- **Fix:**
  - Refuse a step when `!Number.isFinite(terrain)` or `len < radius * 0.5`, and write the checks so NaN fails them (for example `!(above >= -tol)`).
  - Guard `snapToGround` and `besideOf` against zero vectors.
  - On load, fall back to the spawn point when the saved position isn't finite.

## Should-fix
1. **Walking through walls in one packet** (`movement.ts:136`). Confirmed by probe (`move/wall`, re-run by me). Only the step's end point is tested for being inside a prop, so a 1.7 m hop crosses a campwall or fence with 0 corrections on Arcadia and Meridian. Fix: test the path in about 0.25 m steps, like the client.
2. **Honest players get corrected while wading at lake edges.** Server `movement.ts:86` swims below `waterLevel - 1`; client `PlayerController.ts:228` swims below `waterLevel - 1.2`. Confirmed by the helper's probe (`move/wade`: 5, 12 and 7 corrections per pass on Arcadia, Hollowmere and Meridian) and by my reading. Fix: one shared constant.
3. **The Spark medal is lost for good if onboarding finishes in a staged Portus copy** (`onboarding.ts:165-170` with `QuestSystem.ts:711`). I found this by reading; two helpers confirmed it by probe.
   - **Scenario:** for 30 minutes after `stage 1`, a new player who finishes the tutorial gets 50 XP and the completion flag, but no Spark, and onboarding never comes back.
   - **Fix:** exempt Spark from the staging gate.
4. **Load-test NPC kills leave real loot piles in the public world** (`npc/rewards.ts:134,162,189`; no load-test check in `BodyRoom.createPile` at `:830`). Confirmed by the helper's probe (`persist/ltpiles`) and by my reading.
   - Piles belong to a body, not a copy, so `lt:` drops show up in public copies and count toward the 1,000-pile cap.
   - This is relevant now because a load test is running against production. Exposure is low: the bots fire blindly forward near the pad.
   - **Fix:** skip piles when the owner is a load-test bot.
5. **Anyone who finishes the tutorial is never pruned** (`auth/prune.ts:102-107`). Found by reading.
   - Onboarding pays exactly 50 XP (level 2) plus the Spark medal, and both exempt a guest. `prune.test.ts` asserts that levelled and medalled guests are kept.
   - Pruning therefore only removes guests who skipped or quit the tutorial, so the database keeps growing and their names stay taken.
   - **Fix:** ignore `spark` and onboarding-only XP in the prune rule.
6. **Email enumeration still works through other email-OTP endpoints** (`better-auth.ts:71`; only three paths are disabled). Confirmed by the helper's probe (`social/otpenum`).
   - Wrong-code attempts after `request-password-reset` give 403 `TOO_MANY_ATTEMPTS` only for saved addresses, with no email sent.
   - The level-2 path also answers 429 for saved addresses and 200 for unknown ones.
   - **Fix:** disable the unused email-OTP paths and make the error replies identical.
7. **One level-2 session, even a banned one, can drain the global OTP budget** (`accounts.ts:128`). Confirmed by the helper's probe (`social/otpdrain`). Fix: a per-account cap, and exclude banned accounts.
8. **A guest can delete itself and keep its display name forever** (`/delete-anonymous-user` is still enabled; `better-auth.ts:71,128`). Confirmed by the helper's probe (`social/selfdelete`). Fix: set `disableDeleteAnonymousUser: true`.
9. **The rest of S6 (pad cheese):** keep the unreachable clock running when the NPC re-aggros on the same target, or pay nothing for kills made from inside a pad ring.
