## Should-fix
1. **Any prop top can be climbed in one packet** (new; the code predates this diff). Confirmed by probe.
   - **Where:** `apps/server/src/world/movement.ts:124-126,148-149`.
   - **Cause:** a prop top within 0.19 m of the footprint counts as ground, so the step counts as grounded and skips the air-envelope check. Radial motion is free, and the sweep never sees the centre inside the footprint.
   - **Probe `move/climb`:**
     - 2.6 m campwall on Arcadia and Meridian, and 4.2 m pillar on Hollowmere: on top with 0 corrections, then over to the far side.
     - So walls can still be crossed by going over them instead of through.
   - **Probe `opus/perch`:** from a 4.13 m pillar top, a level-20 dusk wolf was killed with player HP 150→150, +138 XP and 18 coins. The wolf never evaded, because:
     - NPC melee needs a height difference of 3 m or less (`npc/attacks.ts:27,73-75`);
     - `outOfReach` uses flat distance (`npc/brain.ts:272-277`), so a perched target is never "out of reach".
   - **Fix:**
     - When the support under the destination is a prop top above the terrain and the start wasn't on that top, also require `len <= airCeiling(a, now)`, as for a landing.
     - Optionally, treat targets more than `HEIGHT_TOL` above an NPC as unreachable.
2. **Requests without a session can still use up the global code budget** (rest of SF7). Confirmed by the helper's probe (`pubdrain`).
   - **Where:** `auth/better-auth.ts:158` → `accounts.ts:146-147`. Public sign-in codes are only capped at 3 per address per hour plus the global day.
   - **Scale:** production `OTP_DAILY_BUDGET=80` (`docs/SPEC.md:805`). One attacker-owned saved address reaches 72 per day on its own.
   - **Probe:** 27 owned addresses, 81 requests from 11 IPs in 750 ms used 80 of 80. A level-2 save then got 429, and a returning player got 200 but no code.
   - **Fix:** separate budgets for public sign-ins and saves, and/or a daily cap per recipient or per IP.

## Nit

- **Level-2 senders share one hourly counter per address** (`accounts.ts:146`, `otp-budget.ts:38-39`): for an hour after an address is saved, 3 sends reveal it (and email the owner). The wrong-code timing gap with a code pending is 0.44 ms on loopback.
- **Load-test identities can take real players' piles**, and the leftovers now vanish (`BodyRoom.ts:239-241,765,862`). This needs the operator's `LOADTEST_SECRET`. Fix: load-test rooms don't list piles, and `pickup` returns early for load-test identities and rooms.
- **A failed kill reward still counts toward repeat decay and pity** (`npc/rewards.ts:79,124`).
- **A failed boss-loot save loses the loot for good** while the medal and Hall entry are still credited (`boss.ts:589`).
- **A throw after the onboarding commit skips the remaining notices** (`onboarding.ts:184`); cosmetic.
- **Chest pile owned by a dead killer** awards `back_for_my_stuff` (`rewards.ts:119`); predates this diff.
- **A dead guest's prune protection** depends on `session_log`, which is deleted after 90 days (`prune.ts:111,120`).
