## Blocker

**B1. Any player can knock any other player off voice, from anywhere, by sending `social:block` repeatedly.** Confirmed by probe and by reading the path end to end.
- **Code path:**
  - `apps/server/src/social/system.ts:222-226` runs every `onBlockHooks` on each `on:true`, even when the target is already blocked.
  - The target is resolved through `liveActor`, so it reaches any room or body (`:218`). The limit is 20 per minute (`:215`).
  - The hook goes `VoiceSystem.ts:104-106` → `blockedPair` (`:425-432`) → `hangUpPair(rotate=true)` (`:435-450`). That revokes the victim's TURN credentials, sets `ts.ice = null` and forces a new mint.
  - Each forced mint uses up the victim's own budget of 10 mints per 10 minutes (`world/voice/turn.ts:97-98,130-139`).
  - Once the budget is gone, `sendIce` sends `available:false` (`VoiceSystem.ts:291-294`), and the client closes every peer connection (`apps/client/src/voice/VoiceClient.ts:358-364`).
- **Scenario:** Eve, without voice enabled, sends `social:block {target: vicUserId}` 11 times. Vic goes through 10 ICE restarts and then gets "relay unavailable" for up to 10 minutes. About one block a minute keeps Vic offline. That includes the owner while filming F6.
- **Probe:** Vic's `voice:ice` sequence was `u1…u10, UNAVAILABLE`, with 10 mints and 10 revokes.
- **Fix:**
  - Rotate only on a new block, and only if the pair actually exchanged signals recently.
  - Allow at most one rotation per target per 10 minutes.
  - Don't charge forced rotations to the victim's mint budget, and keep the old credential if a new one can't be minted.
  - Alternating unblock/block, or using `voice:block` (`VoiceSystem.ts:418-421`), must hit the same guard.

**B2. Boss, camp and beast respawn timers live only in the room instance, so emptying a copy brings the boss straight back.** Confirmed by probe (run twice) and by reading.
- **Code:**
  - `BossController.status` and `respawnAt` are in-memory (`apps/server/src/world/npc/boss.ts:74-75`, set at `:454-455`).
  - A dormant boss spawns as soon as a player comes within range (`:130-137`).
  - The `NpcSystem` spawn groups (`cleared`, `respawnLeft`) and the boss controller are rebuilt for every room (`npc/NpcSystem.ts:93-95`).
  - Rooms auto-dispose (`BodyRoom.ts:99`), and a safe-zone logout leaves at once (`:370`). `play()` then creates a fresh room, reusing copy 0 (`matchmaking.ts:91-96`).
  - SPEC §5.1 says graceful shutdown must "flush boss timers and destruction state", but `onBeforeShutdown` (`BodyRoom.ts:401-409`) saves characters only.
- **Scenario:** a duo kills Tidewrath (2,000 XP, 120 coins, a T2 weapon and tonics each), steps onto a pad and reloads. Tidewrath is back at full HP, which defeats SPEC §6.5's 10-minute respawn per copy. On low-population bodies this happens naturally, and every deploy or restart does the same. Camp chests, the 15-minute beasts, the Golden Hopbit and Ticket #000 reset the same way.
- **Probe:** right after the kill, the boss showed `dead`, respawnIn 599,981 ms. After the last player logged out on pad 0 and rejoined, it showed `alive`, HP 12,000, respawnIn 0.
- **Fix:** keep boss `respawnAt` and spawn-group state in a process-level map keyed by `body:copy`, and rehydrate it in the `NpcSystem` constructor. Persist it in SQLite on shutdown, which also covers SPEC §5.1.

## Should-fix

1. **A block doesn't protect against the blocked player inside parties (stalking, risk R1).** Confirmed by probe.
   - A voice-panel block (`VoiceSystem.ts:411-422`) never calls `parties.cancelBetween`.
   - `parties.accept` (`social/parties.ts:58-86`) and the accept handler (`system.ts:175-182`) don't re-check `blockedEitherWay`.
   - An existing party survives a block. Members get every member's x/y/z, body and room once a second (`system.ts:344-354`), and `partyRoomFor` lets them follow into the same copy.
   - **Probe:** Ann invites Ben, then blocks him from the voice panel. Ben accepts and receives Ann's live coordinates.
   - **Fix:** re-check the block in `accept`, cancel invites from `VoiceSystem.block`, and remove one side from a shared party on any block.

2. **An admin weather override makes the server broadcast the sky state every tick, and lightning goes out of sync.** Confirmed by my probe and the weather agent's.
   - The override sets `slotStart: serverMs - WEATHER_RAMP_MS` (`packages/shared/src/sky.ts:153`). The broadcast key includes `slotStart` (`apps/server/src/world/SkySystem.ts:42-44`), so it changes every tick.
   - **Measured:** 0 messages per second normally; 20 per second (261 B JSON each) during a storm override. That is about 170 KB/s for a 40-player copy, for up to 240 minutes. It happens exactly while staging.
   - Lightning timing is derived from `slotStart` (`sky.ts:186`), so clients that last heard different messages flash at different times.
   - **Fix:** store `since` in `SkyOverride` and use it as `slotStart`, key on `override.until`, and add a test that ticks under an override and expects at most one broadcast.

3. **Staged scenes ignore day/night and weather, and the console can't set the sky (F13).** Confirmed by reading.
   - `runStage` never touches the sky (`apps/server/src/world/quests/stage.ts:93-138`).
   - `parseAdminLine` has no `sky` case (`apps/client/src/ui/panels.ts:445-467`), so `sky storm` sends empty args.
   - `docs/staged-scenes.md` promises "at dusk", golden hour and so on, but a planet is now dark 25% of the time. The only workaround is photo mode's local K/L/O keys in the camera window.
   - **Fix:** give each `StageDef` a `dayT` and weather that `runStage` applies through `SkySystem.onAdmin`. Add `sky …` to the console parser and help text, and update the doc.

4. **Hollowmere's reference look changed: its violet sun turns orange and dimmer.** Confirmed by reading and by recomputing the numbers.
   - The reference elevation is 0.12 (`render/looks.ts:139`). `lightFactors` (`environment.ts:84-89`) then gives dusk 0.86 and sunUp 0.62.
   - The key light becomes 1.12 instead of 1.8, and the colour moves from `ffb0d8` toward the dusk orange.
   - This applies to the bench, `?nodaynight` and the live reference time.
   - **Fix:** measure dusk relative to `look.sunElev`, or add a per-look dusk colour. Add a test that at `referenceDayT` the rig equals `look.sun` and `look.sunIntensity`.

5. **The F10 evidence (59.9 fps p50, below the 60 target) was measured before this lane.**
   - Every fogged fragment now does two sky-LUT samples instead of one (`atmosphere.ts:80-88` through `fogColor`), and the lane adds rain drops, water chop and wetness math.
   - `setBenchMode` forces clear weather (`environment.ts:194-197`).
   - **Fix:** re-run `?bench=g1` on the merged commit, plus one pass with `&weather=storm&time=0`.

6. **Melee NPCs can't reach a combat-tagged player on a pad, but that player can keep shooting.** Confirmed by probe.
   - SAFE stays off while combat-tagged (`BodyRoom.ts:908`), so firing is allowed.
   - NPC steering and charges refuse the ring (`npc/steer.ts:42-52,81`; `attacks.ts:397-401`). `chase` has no "can't reach" exit (`brain.ts:168-178`).
   - **Probe:** the wolf held `chase` at 15.6 m, the player stayed at 150 HP, and the wolf died for 30 XP.
   - **Fix:** forbid firing from inside a pad ring, or make an NPC evade and heal fully after about 5 s with no reachable target.

7. **Fast travel works while combat-tagged.** Confirmed by reading.
   - `QuestSystem.fastTravel` (`quests/QuestSystem.ts:415-434`) checks the pad, discovery and the cooldown, but not `combatUntil`. That contradicts `BodyRoom.ts:907`.
   - With item 6, this is a guaranteed PvP escape.
   - **Fix:** deny while `now < a.combatUntil`.

8. **Boss HP and targeting ignore attackers outside the arena.** Confirmed by probe.
   - HP counts only players inside the arena (`boss.ts:300-311`), and targeting and mechanics use `inArena`, yet damage from outside counts.
   - **Probe:** a sniper 75 m out took the Receiver from 30,000 to 29,652 HP. Max HP stayed at the 1-player value and the sniper was never targeted.
   - **Fix:** count anyone who damaged the boss in the last ~10 s for HP scaling and targeting, or zero damage from beyond radius + 10 m.

9. **An NPC keeps chasing a lingering body after it is removed.** Confirmed by probe.
   - `onPlayerLeave` clears targets only when the client first leaves (`NpcSystem.ts:140-145`). Linger expiry (`BodyRoom.ts:920-923`) and `retire()` call no system hook, and `chase()` drops only dead or SAFE targets (`brain.ts:168`).
   - **Probe:** 21 s after the body vanished, the wolf still chased it and ignored a player shooting it from 8 m.
   - Related: `room.playerById` (`BodyRoom.ts:1035`) excludes lingering bodies, so zap, burst and tag attacks can never hit one.
   - **Fix:** add an `onBodyRemoved` hook, and drop any target that isn't in `sys.live`.

10. **Admin and staged actions write the permanent Hall of Firsts and medals.**
    - Admin `egg` calls `claimEgg`, which calls `engrave` (`QuestSystem.ts:896-901, 702`). Admin-assisted boss kills engrave the first kill (`:776-788`).
    - Staged scene 4 run in production would permanently take "first Receiver kill".
    - **Fix:** skip engraving (and probably medals) for admin-triggered claims and kills, or mark the copy as staged.

11. **Ranged shots pass through boss-arena cover.** Confirmed by reading.
    - Cover is only damaged at a shot's endpoint, through `onPropHit`, and only within radius + 1.5 m of its base (`boss.ts:844-855`).
    - `propOnRay` doesn't know about cover (`combat.ts:53-60`), and `chain()` never calls `onPropHit`.
    - A rocket aimed mid-pillar on the Hollow King's cover flies through, which makes the pillar-topple mechanic much harder than intended.
    - **Fix:** a `raycast` over the cover shapes, used by `propOnRay` and `chain`.

12. **NPC line of sight and NPC bolts ignore breakable props, so cover only works one way.** Confirmed by reading.
    - `canSee` checks terrain only (`npc/attacks.ts:62-69`), and bolts collide only with players and terrain (`:501-528`). The player's own shots are stopped by the same wall.
    - **Fix:** test `host.props?.raycast` in both places.

13. **If the fracture worker fails, breakable props become invisible but still block shots.** Confirmed by probe.
    - `worker.onerror` only logs, so pending promises never settle and the cache keeps the hung promise (`apps/client/src/world/Breakables.ts:48-68`). Nothing draws until `ready`, and the `early` queue grows without limit. Server colliders are always live.
    - **Realistic trigger:** a client deploy while a session is still in Portus. The lazily created worker chunk from the old build then returns 404.
    - **Fix:** settle pending requests with `null` on error, build intact meshes on the main thread as a fallback, and cap `early`.

14. **Some colliders are much bigger than their meshes** (low priority).
    - The fence is a solid 2 × 1.1 m box but is drawn as rails (`shared/planet/breakables.ts:47` vs `client/world/breakableShapes.ts:163-177`). The statue collider is also oversized.
    - The shooter's own tracer isn't clipped by props (`CombatClient.ts:283-293`), so a blocked shot looks like a miss.

15. **Voice-report limits reset on every rejoin.** Confirmed by probe.
    - `reportTimes` and `reportedAt` are per session (`VoiceSystem.ts:154-155,460-466`), and `/api/play` allows 6 joins per 10 s.
    - `/admin` lists only the newest 100 reports (`ops/admin.ts:137`), so a flood pushes real reports off the page.
    - **Fix:** one per-account limiter shared with social `report`, plus paging on `/admin`.

## Nit

- **Weather and day/night:**
  - The key light jumps at sunset and sunrise when it switches between sun and moon (`environment.ts:337`). Ramp the moon in after the switch.
  - Low-tier baked terrain sun is fixed to the reference sun (`world/terrain.worker.ts:118`).
  - `wetness` isn't reset in `setBody` (`environment.ts:172`). Admin `intensity: NaN` passes the clamp (`SkySystem.ts:65`).
  - The rain and wind sound keeps playing when the frame loop stops (`Game.ts:434`).
  - Per-frame allocations: `sliceFor` array (`environment.ts:318`) and the `describe()` string.
  - `WeatherFx.setCapacity` leaks the old buffer (`render/weather.ts:137-145`).
  - Photo-mode storms stop flashing after 60 minutes (`environment.ts:389`).
  - `skySync` has no rate limit (`SkySystem.ts:26`).
  - The admin `dayT` shift never expires.
  - The clock offset is smoothed at 0.3 per sync; snap when the jump is over 5 s.
- **Voice:**
  - A failed 6-hour re-mint throws away credentials that are still valid (`VoiceSystem.ts:311-315`).
  - `voice:block` bypasses the social block cap and rate limit.
  - A blocked client re-dials the blocker every 10 s.
  - Social `report` reaches players outside your copy (`system.ts:234`).
- **Destruction:**
  - A rebuilt prop flickers and plays its burst twice (the `rebuilt` message beats the state patch, `destruction.ts:161-165`).
  - Prop placement differs between V8 and JavaScriptCore in the low bits (28 to 51 props per body, up to 3.4e-13 m). Ids match today; add a cross-engine hash check.
  - Debris seeds are per client (`Breakables.ts:350`), so shatters don't match between clients.
  - The variant count is defined twice. `byId` and `rebuildNear` are unused.
- **Quests:**
  - Stage spawns go through the same uncapped, non-despawning path (`QuestSystem.ts:842-860`). Make sure the egg-NPC fix covers `reason:"stage"`. Stage 5 drops a permanent pile in a public copy.
  - The Golden Hopbit comes back with every pack respawn (`npc/spawns.ts:220`).
  - E8 lamps are shared room-wide, so another player can block someone's sequence.
  - E9 works as a remote escape teleport.
  - The hunt-mission position fallback is dead code.
  - `NpcSystem.tagged` and `QuestSystem.ops` are never pruned.
- **Mine:**
  - The knockback movement allowance (`combat.ts:493`) is clamped away on the next input (`BodyRoom.ts:448`), so repeated Riverstaff or rocket shoves can cause snap-backs.
  - No reserved display names (`db/names.ts`). "Admin", "FIRSTLIGHT" and "Moderator" are free to take.
  - Guest accounts are never pruned. auth.db and game.db grow without bound, and names can be squatted.
  - DB paths default to `./data` in production (`env.ts:17-18`), outside the volume. Add them to the boot check in `main.ts:20-31`.
  - The global OTP budget of 1,000/day can be drained by a sender spread over about 125 IPs or /64s. Consider requiring a session at level 2 or higher before sending.
  - `respawn` and `travel` write to SQLite on every message, outside the 5/s bucket (`BodyRoom.ts:179,207`). This extends the earlier unthrottled-message nit.
- **Addendum to Codex S4 (props don't block movement):** standing inside a prop makes a player immune to hitscan and projectiles, because rays from inside stop at t = 0. That raises the priority of the existing fix.

## Checked, OK
- **Auth:**
  - `signJWT` and `verifyJWT` are server-only in Better Auth 1.7.7, and the JWT payload is limited to `{name, guest}`.
  - Load-test tokens are honoured only when `LOADTEST_SECRET` is set.
  - `update-user`, `change-email` and `delete-user` are disabled.
  - OTP codes are hashed, with 3 attempts and a 5-minute expiry. Rate limits key IPv6 on /64.
  - The origin-secret middleware strips the client-IP header before setting it. Production refuses dev guests, a missing secret and a short auth secret.
- **`/admin` and `/stats`:**
  - Pages are escaped, have a no-script CSP, frame-deny headers and an Origin check on POST, and nothing mutates on GET.
  - The claim token is single-use and requires a saved account.
- **Persistence:** migrations are forward-only. Saves only happen if the account row exists. Pile takes are transactional. Account merges move every table.
- **Colyseus:** `onAuth` rejects, only `reconnect` is exposed, CORS uses an allow-list, `maxPayload` is 16 KB, and the 60 msg/s cap drops the client into reconnect.
- **Assets worker:** static files only, with `/v/*` immutable.
- **Sky:** a pure function of epoch ms, with no gameplay effect from lightning, and works on the WebGL2 fallback.
- **Earlier render review:** all its items are fixed except the per-frame blob array.
- **Licence and XSS:** three-pinata is used only through npm (MIT). Every player-controlled string goes through `textContent`.
- **Hygiene:** event and listener cleanup is per room.
